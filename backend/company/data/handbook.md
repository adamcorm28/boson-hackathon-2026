# Dunder Mifflin Scranton: branch handbook

## About the branch
The Scranton branch of Dunder Mifflin Paper Company sells paper and office supplies to businesses, schools and local government across northeastern Pennsylvania. Regional Manager: Michael Scott. Sales run about $700k a month at a 22% gross margin. Customers order through their sales rep or online through Dunder Mifflin Infinity.

## Products
Copy paper (letter, legal, tabloid), cardstock, cotton bond letterhead, envelopes, labels, receipt rolls, and basic office supplies. Our best seller is 20 lb letter copy paper at $45 per case (10 reams per case).

## Pricing and bulk discount
List price applies to orders under 50 cases. Orders of 50 cases or more get a 10% bulk discount on the whole order. Example: 60 cases at $45 is $2,700 before the discount, $270 off, $2,430 total. Sales reps may quote the bulk rate in advance, and Dunder Mifflin Infinity must charge the same price the rep quoted.

## Dunder Mifflin Infinity
Infinity is the branch's online ordering website, built and run by Ryan Howard's engineering team. About 18% of orders come through it. Known issue: the checkout currently does not apply the bulk discount (DM-101). That is blocking the Lackawanna County Schools contract.

## Org chart and team
Michael Scott (Regional Manager / CEO) manages Kevin Malone (Accounting / CFO), Ryan Howard (Engineering Lead, Dunder Mifflin Infinity) and Jim Halpert (Sales Lead). Dwight Schrute (Engineer) reports to Ryan. Other sales reps include Stanley Hudson and Phyllis Vance.

## Q4 goals
Beat the Stamford branch on Q4 sales. The Q4 target is $2.25M; the branch is at $520k so far, slightly behind Stamford's $541k. The biggest single lever is closing the Lackawanna County Schools contract (about $120k a year).

## Customer service policy
Respond to customer emails within one business day. Pricing complaints go to the sales rep and, if the website is involved, to the Infinity engineering team.

## Office rules
Fire drills must be announced in advance. Pretzel Day is in the spring. The conference room whiteboard holds the branch task board.
